TRINGGG: QR STICKERS

HOW TO ADD: unzip into the repo ROOT (creates the qr/ folder).
Print: open qr/print.html, press Ctrl+P, cut along the dashed lines.
Gate QR -> citizen dashboard with the Gate ID filled in.
Bag QR  -> same, plus that bag's button glows (one tap to request).
The QR codes point to https://nikhil-0268.github.io/automatic-octo-parakeet/
They ONLY work after GitHub Pages is on: Settings -> Pages -> Deploy from a branch ->
main, / (root) -> Save. If the repo is renamed, the QR codes must be regenerated.
