HOW TRINGGG WORKS

A smarter way to keep every street clean.

A simple connection between households and the collection companies already serving them. No complicated app required for residents using basic phones.

01 / REQUEST

Ring. Ready. Done.

Residents give a missed call, use Nepali voice prompts or tap one button to tell tringgg they are ready for collection.

02 / DISPATCH

The right vehicle, on route.

Available collectors see approved requests on their route, with smart grouping to help reduce unnecessary trips.

03 / VERIFY

Scan. Collect. Reward.

Collectors scan a QR at the gate, confirm segregation, and award Green Points for verified responsible pickups.

THE MISSION

Technology that works for every household.

From keypad phones to collection-truck dashboards, tringgg is designed around accessible service, accountable pickup records and cleaner neighborhoods.
